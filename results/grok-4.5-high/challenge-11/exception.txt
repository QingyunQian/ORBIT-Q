Use an open-boundary spin-1 chain with local basis ordered as `|+1>`, `|0>`, `|-1>`. The local spin operators are

```text
Sx = (1 / sqrt(2)) [[0, 1, 0], [1, 0, 1], [0, 1, 0]]
```

## Variational Ansatz


### 1. Single-Site Rotation Block


```text
Rz(phi) = exp(-i phi Sz),
```

### 2. Even-Bond Entangling Block
